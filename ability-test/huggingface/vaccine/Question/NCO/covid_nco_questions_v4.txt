Question 1
[Background]
We compare two patient groups within the same cohort regarding COVID-19 vaccination:
- Treatment: Individuals vaccinated with BNT162b2 (Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine)
- Control: Individuals with no documented COVID-19 vaccination.
[Task Description]
You’ll receive an outcome and the percentage of affected patients in each group.
Decide whether vaccination has a causal effect on that outcome.
You will receive a query describing an outcome and the proportion of patients in each group who experienced it:
- One percentage for patients who received the COVID-19 vaccine (treatment group).
- One percentage for patients who were unvaccinated (control group).
Your task is to determine whether vaccination has a causal effect on that outcome.
[Cohort Description]
Adults aged 18 years or older observed between January 1 and December 31, 2022.
The treatment group received the first dose of BNT162b2 (Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 vaccine) during this period, while the control group remained unvaccinated.
All individuals had no prior COVID-19 infection or vaccination before the study start and had at least one healthcare visit within 18 months to 7 days before the index date.
[Instruction]
- Judge whether vaccination causally influences the outcome.
- Note that both groups come from a large population cohort (13,635 vaccinated and 891,968 unvaccinated adults), and event rates for most outcomes are generally low — for example, below 10% or even 1%. A low or high absolute event rate does not necessarily imply the absence or presence of a causal relationship.
- "Yes" = Positive Outcome (OOI); "No" = Negative Control Outcome (NCO).
- Reply with only "yes" or "no".
[Few-shot examples]
- Treatment group using BNT162b2 (treatment) had 1.67% astigmatism, compared with 0.66% for control group. Does treatment have causal effect to the astigmatism (is it a Positive Outcome)? → no.
- Treatment group using BNT162b2 (treatment) had 0.08% autism or autistic disorder, compared with 0.11% for control group. Does treatment have causal effect to the autism or autistic disorder (is it a Positive Outcome)? → no.
- Treatment group using BNT162b2 (treatment) had 0.16% concussion, compared with 0.26% for control group. Does treatment have causal effect to the concussion (is it a Positive Outcome)? → no.
- Treatment group using BNT162b2 (treatment) had 0.32% Alcohol use disorder, compared with 0.78% for control group. Does treatment have causal effect to the Alcohol use disorder (is it a Positive Outcome)? → yes.
- Treatment group using BNT162b2 (treatment) had 4.46% Anxiety disorders, compared with 5.73% for control group. Does treatment have causal effect to the Anxiety disorders (is it a Positive Outcome)? → yes.
- Treatment group using BNT162b2 (treatment) had 0.30% Behavioral and emotional disorders with onset usually occurring in childhood and adolescence, compared with 0.71% for control group. Does treatment have causal effect to the Behavioral and emotional disorders with onset usually occurring in childhood and adolescence (is it a Positive Outcome)? → yes.
[Your Task]
[Question]
