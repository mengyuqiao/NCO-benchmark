- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 0.13% Acquired keratoderma, compared with 0.08% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Acquired keratoderma (is it a Positive Outcome)? - no
- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 94.81% Type 2 diabetes mellitus, compared with 96.68% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Type 2 diabetes mellitus (is it a Positive Outcome)? - yes
