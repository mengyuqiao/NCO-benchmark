Question 1
[Background]
We compare two treatments for type 2 diabetes:
- Treatment: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (GLP-1RA)
- Control: Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter-2 Inhibitors (SGLT2i)
[Task Description]
You’ll receive an outcome and the percentage of affected patients in each group.
Decide whether these treatments have a causal effect on that outcome.
You will receive a query describing an outcome and the proportion of patients in each group who experienced it:
- One percentage for patients using GLP-1RA (treatment group).
- One percentage for patients using SGLT2i (control group).
Your task is to determine whether the treatments (GLP-1RA and SGLT2i) have a causal effect on that outcome.
[Cohort Description]
Adults with type 2 diabetes who began either GLP-1RA or SGLT2i.
All had a prior diabetes diagnosis.
GLP-1RA users: mean age 58.0, BMI 37.4.
SGLT2i users: mean age 64.6, BMI 32.8.
Charlson Comorbidity Index ≥ 3 in 65.2% (GLP-1RA) and 68.5% (SGLT2i).
[Instruction]
- Judge whether the treatments causally influence the outcome.
- Both treatments are expected to behave similarly (either both yes or both no).
- “Yes” = Positive Outcome (OOI); “No” = Negative Control Outcome (NCO).
- Reply with only “yes” or “no.”
[Few-shot Examples]
- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 0.06% Foreign body in orifice, compared with 0.09% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Foreign body in orifice (is it a Positive Outcome)? - no
- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 0.01% Toxic effect of lead compound, compared with 0.04% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Toxic effect of lead compound (is it a Positive Outcome)? - no
- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 1.29% Epidermoid cyst, compared with 0.58% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Epidermoid cyst (is it a Positive Outcome)? - no
- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 78.37% Hypertension, compared with 84.71% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Hypertension (is it a Positive Outcome)? - yes
- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 78.09% Hyperlipidemia, compared with 84.45% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Hyperlipidemia (is it a Positive Outcome)? - yes
- Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 64.14% Obesity, compared with 44.50% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Obesity (is it a Positive Outcome)? - yes
[Your Task]
[Question]
Treatment group using GLP-1RA (treatment) had 27.35% Major depressive disorder, compared with 21.44% for control group using SGLT2i. Does treatment have causal effect to the Major depressive disorder (is it a Positive Outcome)?
Answer: [Your answer, "yes" or "no"]
